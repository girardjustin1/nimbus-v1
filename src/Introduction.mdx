# DAS prototypes

The **Deal Activation System** (DAS) — how a publisher sets up, manages and reports on
direct-sold campaigns — worked through six review rounds with product, from 22 September
to 6 November 2026. Each round is a clickable prototype, frozen at the version that was
reviewed, so any two can be compared side by side. The Nimbus tokens, tables, fields and
type rules settled here became the style guide for the whole dashboard.
