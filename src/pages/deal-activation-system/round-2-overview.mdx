# Round 2 · October 1

Round 2 lives in the prototype rather than in Storybook — the whole point of it is the
loop between the libraries and campaign setup, which only makes sense when you can walk
it. <S>Open the prototype →</S>

## What changed

**One rail, not two.** The left "On this page" rail repeated what the right rail already
said and ate the width the form needed. The right rail now does both jobs: it summarises
the campaign and jumps you to anything that needs fixing.

**Two rail variants, because the answer isn't ours to give.**
<S to="#/rail-summary">Summary → Review → Publish</S> matches the backend round-trip DAS
does today; Publish only lights up once the check comes back clean, and any edit resets
it. <S to="#/rail-validation">Live validation → Publish</S> assumes the client can
validate on its own. That's the question for engineering.

**Targeting is five modules at one level** — Geos, Platform, Apps, Ad Unit, Keywords —
and the <S to="#/step-targeting">geo and app pickers actually work</S> rather than being
states.

**Two sections the product has and the prototype never did.**
<S to="#/asset-setup">Asset Setup</S> makes a creative by pasting markup, with the format
animation rendering beside it as you choose a size.
<S to="#/keyword-health">Keyword Health</S> answers the question the charter implies but
never addresses: are your keywords actually arriving? Both are reachable from inside a
campaign and return you to it with the thing you just made attached.

**Click to fill.** Every empty field populates on click, and the toolbar fills a page.
Switch the toggle to **Bad** and fill again to produce the error states on demand.

**Commentary moved out of the screens.** Concept notes and the walkthrough live behind
the toolbar's **Info** button, so a screenshot of a screen is a screenshot of the design.

## Terminology

Round 2 uses the product's own words. Where we had to invent one, it carries an
<Chip tone="added">Added</Chip> chip on screen. The source of every term is recorded in
`src/pages/deal-activation-system/terminology.ts`.

**Product** was read verbatim off the real dashboard. **Inferred** came from the Review
page's read-only row labels, because the input screens were never captured — the wording
is real, but the input label could differ. **Added** has no equivalent in DAS today.

## The one that needs deciding

`Ad Type` and `Ad Unit` are different things in the product and sit near each other:
**Ad Type** is how a creative is encoded (`HTML`, `VAST (xml)`) and lives on an asset;
**Ad Unit** is which placement a campaign targets. Round 1 called the second one "Ad unit
type", which reads like the first. We've adopted the product's names, but the collision
is the product's, and it's worth raising.
