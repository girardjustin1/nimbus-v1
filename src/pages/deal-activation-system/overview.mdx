# Deal Activation System — Extended Targeting

<div style={{ background: "#F9FAFB", border: "1px solid #EAECF0", borderRadius: 14, padding: "16px 20px", margin: "8px 0 24px", fontSize: 15, lineHeight: 1.65, color: "#344054" }}>
  <strong>The experience.</strong> A publisher sets up a direct-sold campaign on a single page instead of a five-step wizard,
  with problems flagged as they go and Publish enabled only when everything checks out. They target it with their own
  keywords (words their app already sends, like "sports" or "over21") plus ad unit type and device language, and manage
  those keywords in a new Keyword Library. After launch, every campaign shows at a glance whether it's on pace to deliver
  what was promised to the advertiser, and DAS results are reported separately from Open Marketplace revenue.
</div>

**The goal:** let publishers run high-value direct campaigns through Nimbus instead of GAM.
DAS gains **publisher-defined keyword targeting** plus two new standard targets (**ad unit
type** and **device language**), and the setup, management and reporting screens need to
handle that extra granularity.

Everything in this section is an exploratory **screen concept** built from the Nimbus design
system with fictional sample data. Each screen opens with a dashed teal note explaining what
the concept is testing. That note is a review annotation, not product UI.

## What we're designing for

- **Keywords are opaque.** The publisher's app sends words or codes (`sports`, `over21`,
  `77541`) in `user.keywords`. Nimbus stores and matches them case-insensitively and never
  interprets them.
- **Create → target → report.** Publishers need to create, edit and delete keywords, target
  campaigns with them (ANY by default, or ALL), and see results.
- **Think in ad units, not creative sizes.** One creative *type* per campaign. Other formats
  or languages use **Publish & Duplicate**.
- **Delivery is a promise.** Under-delivering a direct deal is a contract problem, so spend
  vs budget vs flight needs to be visible at a glance.
- **Reporting guardrail.** Aggregate keyword metrics in the dashboard (keyword-targeted
  yes/no + matched keyword count). Per-keyword detail via CSV/API only, and no per-keyword
  charts at launch.
- **Out of scope:** position, session depth, device type, day parting, third-party
  audiences, API campaign setup, creative/size targeting.

## Open questions

<ol style={{ listStyle: "decimal", paddingLeft: 24, lineHeight: 1.7 }}>
  <li>Is the keyword library per publisher account or per app?</li>
  <li>Can a keyword used by a live campaign be renamed? (Concepts assume no: add new, switch, delete.)</li>
  <li>Should we suggest keywords already seen in traffic that aren't in the library yet?</li>
  <li>Is ANY/ALL enough, or will publishers ask for exclusions? (Exclusions are currently unsupported.)</li>
  <li>Does the one-pager still need an explicit review moment, or is the summary rail enough?</li>
  <li>Is CSV-only per-keyword reporting a hard launch rule or a performance safeguard we can revisit?</li>
</ol>
