# Campaign Setup

One page where a publisher sets up a direct-sold campaign: the deal it belongs to, how it
competes in the auction, what it costs, when it runs, who it reaches and what it shows. A
rail on the right summarises the campaign as it fills in and ends in Review.

## Rules

**Type**

- Proxima Nova throughout.
- Headlines are extra bold (800).
- Labels are bold (700) and black: field labels, radio and checkbox options, and the
  titles of targeting cards.
- Nothing is smaller than 15px.

**Layout**

- An instruction sits under its heading or label, **above** the control, so an open list
  can't cover it. Errors go **under** the control they're about.
- A field reads label, instruction, control: <S id="controls-field--default">Field</S>.
- Option cards (Auction Rules, Ad Unit) sit two across and are clickable end to end. They
  share the text fields' edge: a light ring and a small shadow.
- Targeting cards close up under their title when they open with an instruction:
  <S id="controls-target-card--with-instruction">Target Card</S>.
- The summary rail is 300px wide and stays in view while the form scrolls.

**Behaviour**

- Seven fields pull from a list: deal, priority, frequency cap, geos, apps, keywords and
  creative. They behave the same way:
  - click or press ↓ for the whole list, A–Z;
  - type to narrow it;
  - ↑ ↓ and Enter to pick.
- Geos, Apps, Keywords and Creative panels close with **Done**, Escape or a click away.
- Picks land in a table, not chips. Geos keeps a tree, because taking a whole region is
  the common case.
- Dates only. A flight runs from the start of its first day to the end of its last, UTC.
- Review checks the campaign. Publish and Publish & Duplicate live in the Review modal.
  Once a campaign is published, it offers **Duplicate**.

## Copy

Staging's wording is transcribed in `prototypes/das/v3/screens/copy-deck.ts`. In the
prototype, the toolbar's **Copy** switch shows:

- **Original:** staging's wording only. Anything staging has no copy for is left out, so
  gaps show.
- **New:** Prototype 3's wording, as built. You can highlight what is new or hide it.

Final wording is Product's to write.

## Open

- **Which rail.** Summary → Review → Publish matches today's backend check. Live
  validation → Publish assumes the client can validate on its own. That is an engineering
  question. Both rails are in <S id="page--empty">Page</S> and <S id="rail-campaign-summary--empty">Campaign Summary</S>.
- **Daily Impression Cap** is removed until there's a decision about what it counts.
- **Copy volume** is still to be cut, by Product.
- **Manage campaigns** is being designed separately.

Earlier rounds and the Studio concept are hidden from this sidebar but still open by
direct link.
