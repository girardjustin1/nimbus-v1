# Round 6 · 6 Nov review guide

On 6 November Product came back from showing DAS to sales with two asks. Sales liked the
campaign overview's **pacing chart** but worried it could make Nimbus look bad when a
campaign falls behind, so the chart keeps its place and gains **context**: the ad
requests and daily active users behind each day. And **every table sorts**. Both are
built into prototype **v6**; nothing else in Round 6 moved.

<div style={{ background: "#E6F6F6", borderLeft: "4px solid #1F7F80", borderRadius: 10, padding: "12px 16px", margin: "16px 0", fontSize: 15, color: "#101828" }}>
  <b>Decided, 6 Nov:</b> chart <b>A</b> — actual numbers on one chart, nothing against normal. The prototype shows A on every campaign; the other concepts below are kept for reference. And no coaching: the yellow pacing strip is gone, so the overview only states the data.
</div>
