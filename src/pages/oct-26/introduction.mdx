# Round 5 · review guide

On 26 October Product approved DAS — setup and reporting both. What was left was visual:
make DAS read as part of the Nimbus dashboard, and write the rules down so the
Performance Insights redesign can reuse them. This guide walks every Round 5 change **in
the order to review it**, the way a publisher moves through DAS: the campaigns list, a
campaign, its full performance, then the libraries, then setup. It finishes with the
components every screen now shares, and what's kept or still open.

Each step has a screenshot of v5, what changed and why (in Product's words from the
call), how to check it, and links: the screen in the **prototype**, **Round 4 → 5** side
by side, and the **components** it uses.
