## The process agreed

1. **Finish Storybook** so it shows the styles we want, before any porting.
2. **Engineering ports** everything from Storybook into the codebase.
3. **Staging:** push, QA and fine-tune, then roll out section by section.

Changing styles after the port creates drift, so review happens here first.
