**Asked for** means there is a choice to make and two proposals to compare.
**Applied** means it was decided on the call and is already in prototype 3 — recorded
here anyway, because removing something is much easier to reverse when you can still see
what was removed.

## The two proposals, in one sentence each

**A — the search sits on the page.** Fewest clicks, and what you are building stays
visible while you build it. Costs vertical space on a form that already has seven
sections.

**B — the search is a modal.** The section stays short whatever happens, there is room
for checkboxes so several things go in at once, and the Add button answers the other
complaint from the call: that clicking away to commit is a strange way to save.

Both end in the same table, so whichever is chosen the other costs nothing to abandon.

## Deliberately not in this round

- **Geos.** Only the click-to-commit was objected to, and the right fix is whatever
  keywords and creative settle on. Changing it twice would be worse than changing it late.
- **Batch actions.** The product stages bulk edits — *“Preparing 1,988 changes”* — and
  reviews them before committing, where ours apply immediately. Worth reconciling, not
  this round.
- **Manage campaigns grouped by campaign rather than by deal.** Flagged on the call as
  the next session's subject.
- **Categories or folders for keywords.** Asked directly, answered directly: *“For V1, no.”*

## Rounds 1 and 2 are untouched

Everything here changes prototype 3 only. Round 1 is the record of what the Extended

change needed a shared file, prototype 3 got its own copy rather than editing theirs.
