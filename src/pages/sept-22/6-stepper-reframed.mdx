# 6 · The stepper is recorded, not recommended

<ItemDoc n={6} />

## What actually changes

Nothing on the screens. This is a labelling and framing change, and it matters because of
how the two concepts currently sit next to each other: one page and step-by-step read as
two live options, when the question between them has already been answered.

- The step-by-step concept keeps all its screens.
- Its overview says plainly that it is the alternative that was explored and set aside,
  and why — a customer described a wizard as cumbersome the week before the review.
- One-page setup is named as the direction.

## Why not delete it

Because the fact that a wizard was built, looked at, and turned down is worth more than a
folder with one fewer entry in it. Six months from now, "did we consider a step-by-step
flow?" is a question someone will ask, and the answer should be a link rather than a
recollection.

There is also a real chance the stepper is right for a narrower case — a first-time setup,
or a guided path for an account that has never booked a direct deal. Keeping it costs
nothing and leaves that door open.
