# 7 · The device preview says it is an approximation

<ItemDoc n={7} />

## Why a caption is the whole fix

The worry raised on the call was not that the preview is inaccurate — it is that it looks
accurate enough to be held to. A publisher who reads it as exact will report every
difference on a real handset as a bug, and the more compelling the preview is, the harder
that gets to walk back.

Nothing about the rendering needs to change for that. The claim does:

> This preview shows **how the ad renders** in a Nimbus-served app.

becomes

> **Approximate preview.** Size and placement vary by device, screen and app.

## Where the caveat has to live

Not in a tooltip, and not only in the docs. Previews get screenshotted and pasted into
threads, and the caveat has to survive that trip — so it sits in the frame, in the
screenshot, next to the thing it is qualifying.
