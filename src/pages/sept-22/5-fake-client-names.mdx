# 5 · Sample advertisers stay fictional

<ItemDoc n={5} />

## The advertisers in these screens

Summit Sportswear, Harvest Brewing, GreenThumb Supply, Trailhead Outfitters and the
publisher account Pocket Garden Media are all invented, as are every deal ID, budget,
spend figure and keyword. None of it comes from real reporting.

That is not a gap waiting to be filled in before a demo. It is the reason these screens
can be shown to any customer without a conversation about whose numbers they are looking
at — which is exactly the use the review had in mind.

## Why this is written down

This is the kind of decision that gets undone by someone being helpful. Swapping in a real
advertiser makes a prototype feel more concrete for one demo, and quietly makes it
unshowable for every demo after that.

So the rule goes where someone is likely to be standing when the thought occurs: the
header comment of the data file, and the README next to the prototype links.

These prototypes also publish to a **public URL**, which makes the rule load-bearing
rather than merely tidy.
