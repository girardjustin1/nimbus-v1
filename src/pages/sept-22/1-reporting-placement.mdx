## The part that is not in Storybook

Moving the Storybook category is half of it. The other half is the product nav: these
screens rendered with **performance insights** highlighted, so anyone looking at a
screenshot read them as a Performance Insights proposal no matter where the story lived.

The revised screen highlights a **reporting** entry under Deal Activation System instead.
That entry does not exist in the product today — it is proposed here, the same way
**keyword library** is proposed in the round one concepts. If it is the wrong shape, it is
a cheap thing to change; leaving the nav saying Performance Insights is not.
