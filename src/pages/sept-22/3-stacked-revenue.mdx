# 3 · Revenue by day stacks by campaign

<ItemDoc n={3} />

## The part that needed a judgement call

Stacking the campaigns inside the full-height bar does what was asked and does not work.
DAS is about **14% of total revenue**, so five campaigns split across that share come out
two or three pixels tall each — strictly harder to read than the single teal block they
replaced. Doing it literally would have satisfied the request and missed the point of it.

So the chart has two views, and the data behind them is identical:

- **DAS campaigns** — scaled to DAS, where the campaigns are actually legible. Default.
- **Share of total** — the same stack with Open Marketplace back on top, which is the
  comparison the original chart existed to make.

Worth a look together, since "leave Open Marketplace" and "make the campaigns visible"
pull against each other at this ratio. If the split view is the wrong answer, the other
obvious one is a second, shorter chart underneath rather than a toggle.

## Keeping the numbers honest

Two things could have drifted here, and both are closed off.

**Which campaigns exist.** The series are derived from the same `campaigns` array the
delivery table reads, filtered the same way — scheduled campaigns have not run, so they
contribute nothing to a past window. Add a campaign to the data and it appears in both
places or neither.

**What the totals say.** Each day's per-campaign values add up to exactly the `das` figure
that was already there, so **DAS revenue $35,250** and **share of total 14%** are the same
numbers that were reviewed. Only the breakdown is new.

The split itself is a fixed weighting with a deterministic wobble — not a random number —
so the chart is identical on every render and in every screenshot taken from it.

## Colour

Dark to light teal, biggest campaign darkest. The DAS block still reads as one mass
against the gray Open Marketplace band, which is what makes the share comparison survive
being subdivided. Open Marketplace itself is untouched.
