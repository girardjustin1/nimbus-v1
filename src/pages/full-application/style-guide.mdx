## 4 · The reporting page

Every reporting page — realtime, Performance Insights, buyer breakdown, benchmarks, discrepancy reports, Full performance — shares one shape, top to bottom:

1. **Date row** — START DATE / END DATE cells, the quick range ("Last 7 days"), the interval ("By Day"), "All reports are in UTC." and the page's actions (SAVE QUERY, DOWNLOAD .CSV).
2. **Bands** — full-width labelled rows: Metrics, Breakdown, Filters (+ ADD), Compare, Chart Type.
3. **Summary tiles** — the headline figures for what's ticked.
4. **Chart** — the chosen chart type.
5. **Report table** — the same data, sortable.

## 6 · Charts

- Charts show **actual numbers**. Nothing is indexed against "normal" (Product, 6 Nov).
- Series colours come from one checked palette (teal, pink, navy, brown, purple, orange, grey for "Other"); at most eight slices, the rest folded into "Other".
- Axis ticks, legends and tooltips at 15px; the tooltip names the day in full.
- Two axes only where staging already uses them and the series are real figures.

<Pill kind="story" href="./?path=/docs/application-ui-charts--docs">Charts</Pill>

## 7 · Copy

- **Staging's wording** for labels, tabs, buttons and columns. Restyle, don't rename.
- **No coaching.** A page states what the data is and never tells the publisher what to do (Product, 6 Nov): no warning strips with targets, no "you should", no "contact your rep".
- **No added explanations** where staging has none. Deliberate one-offs (the DAS ad unit selector with descriptions) stay one-offs.

## 8 · Sample data

Everything in the prototypes and Storybook is **fictional** — publisher, apps, demand partners, people, URLs — because this Storybook is public. The reporting week is Nov 7 to Nov 13, 2026 on every page.
