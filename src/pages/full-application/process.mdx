<Callout>
  Why Storybook comes first: changing styles after the port creates drift, because every tweak then has to go back
  and forth between Storybook and the code. Review happens here, once.
</Callout>

## Priorities

1. **Performance Insights.** One of the oldest parts of the dashboard, and it holds most of the base styles the
   rest use: the top filters and date bar, the stacked sections, charts, tables and the summary tiles.
2. **The Deal Activation System,** already worked through six review rounds.
3. **Everything that shares Performance Insights' chunks** — buyer breakdown, benchmarks, activity, floors, manage
   campaigns — followed by the rest of the dashboard.

Within DAS, engineering's order of difficulty is dropdowns, then modals, then tables.
