# How to review

The next step agreed on 13 November: compare staging and the full application side by
side on the pages that matter most, and say what to keep from each. This page sets that
up.

## Set up

1. Open **staging** in one window and the **full application** in another, both at about 1440px wide.
2. In the full application, use the sidebar exactly as on staging — every item leads to its page. The screen menu at the
   top (and **All screens**) also lists every state: empty, error, no permission, each chart type.
3. Go through the pages below in order. Each one keeps staging's structure and wording, so what differs is styling.
