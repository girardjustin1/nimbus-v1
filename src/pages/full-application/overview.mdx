## Rules every screen follows

- **One typeface, three weights.** Proxima Nova; headlines 800, labels 700, nothing under 15px.
- **Two kinds of table.** Reports get the charcoal header, lists a light one. Every column sorts.
- **One reporting page.** Date row, bands, summary tiles, chart, report table, in that order.
- **Staging's wording.** Restyle, don't rename.
- **No coaching.** A page states what the data is and never tells the publisher what to do.
- **Actual numbers.** Charts show real figures, nothing indexed against "normal".
- **Fictional data.** Every publisher, partner, person and figure is made up; this Storybook is public.
