# Round 6 · review guide

On 28 October Product walked DAS reporting with engineering. Customers' complaint is
that DAS and Performance Insights show **two different spend numbers**, so the decision
was to show one: the trued-up figure, labelled “Subject to true-up”, everywhere. What
stays live is **pacing**. This guide walks every Round 6 change **in the order to review
it**: the campaigns list, a campaign, then its full performance.

Each step has a screenshot of v6, what changed and why (in the words from the call), how
to check it, and links: the screen in the **prototype**, and **Round 5 → 6** side by side.

<Step n={1} title="Campaigns · Pacing column">

<Shot src={s01} caption="v6 · Deal Activation System › manage campaigns" />

<Changed>
  <ul>
    <li><b>Campaign Health is Pacing</b> — on the chip, in the overview, the filter and the tooltips.</li>
    <li><b>Pacing is a column</b> beside Status: the colour, the percentage and its word (On target, Behind, At risk).</li>
    <li><b>It sorts worst first.</b> One click puts at-risk campaigns at the top, then behind, then on target. Campaigns that aren't pacing — queued, paused, complete, Fallback — say why in grey and always sort last.</li>
    <li><b>Budget and Spend stay.</b> On the call they were going to go; once spend became the trued-up number everywhere, they stay, with the true-up note on Spend.</li>
  </ul>
</Changed>

<Shot src={s02} caption="Sorted by pacing — the campaigns that need attention at the top" />

<Said>Let's have a column called pacing … let's elevate it to this page so you can sort by pacing, and then all the ones that [need help] will come up to the top.</Said>

<Check>
  <ol>
    <li>Click the <b>Pacing</b> header once: at risk, then behind, then on target. Click again to reverse — the campaigns not pacing stay at the bottom either way.</li>
    <li>Click the <b>Pacing</b> card: the table narrows to behind and at risk.</li>
  </ol>
</Check>

<Step n={2} title="One spend number">

<Shot src={s03} caption="Hovering the ⓘ beside Total spend" />

<Changed>
  <ul>
    <li><b>Spend is the trued-up number</b> Performance Insights shows, so DAS never shows a second figure.</li>
    <li><b>Every spend figure carries an ⓘ:</b> “Subject to true-up. Updated hourly. The last 48 hours can still be corrected, so this can move a little up or down.” On Total spend and the Spend column here, the Spend card and delivery rail on a campaign, and Revenue in Full performance.</li>
    <li><b>Nothing says real time.</b> The delivery rail's “Live delivery” heading is now “Delivery”. The only live figure in DAS is pacing.</li>
  </ul>
</Changed>

<Said>The actual specific numbers that we're hanging our hat on are all the same across the whole platform, and we label every single thing subject to true-up.</Said>
<Said>Showing 2 different numbers is just a big no-no.</Said>

<Step n={3} title="Campaign Delivery · concept">

<Status tone="Concept" /> Built to explore, not decided — to talk through together.

<Shot src={s04} caption="Concept · Campaign Delivery beside Status (full page)" />

<Changed>
  <ul>
    <li><b>One column, two cells.</b> While a campaign is in flight they read as one: “In flight · on target · 102.0% pacing”, or behind, or at risk.</li>
    <li><b>Once it's complete</b> the same cells settle to its final Budget and Spend — two columns so each still sorts. A badly paced campaign doesn't stay red forever (Back to school, at the bottom).</li>
    <li><b>Sorting:</b> Campaign Delivery sorts by pacing, worst first; Budget and Spend sort the completed campaigns, with those in flight after them.</li>
  </ul>
</Changed>

<Said>This column could change to campaign delivery. And when the campaign is in flight, it can say in-flight pacing on target or in-flight pacing behind.</Said>
<Said>At least the bad visual goes away and it just settles to budget and spend.</Said>
